How it Works

1. Upload Media: Drag and drop your favorite video or image into the canvas. Our platform handles processing completely in your browser, keeping your files secure and private.

2. Choose Rendering: Select from various artistic modes like ASCII, Halftone, or Bitmap. Customize the character sets and typography to define the texture and visual density of your artwork.

3. Stylize & Tweak: Use the color pickers to apply unique palettes. Adjust brightness, contrast, and gamma to pull out the best details from your original media.

4. Export & Share: Once satisfied with your real-time preview, hit export to generate a high-fidelity video or image output, ready for social media or your personal portfolio.
